# PoRacer cleanup contract

- `js/poracer/index.js` explicitly owns input, the snapshot timeline, the animation frame and the audio voices; `renderer.js` draws (2D canvas only), `audio.js` sounds, `input.js` reads keys, touch and a gamepad. Start/stop release listeners, animation frames, oscillators and cached bitmaps. There is no WebGL pass and no `compositor.js` any more: the effects that `effectsReduced()` had made unreachable since 2026-09-17 (shake, sparks, weather, speed lines, fog, bloom, the GL post pass) were deleted on 2026-09-30, about 450 lines. `audio.js` feeds `PoAudioBus`'s sfx bus and must never close the shared context. Weather came back on 2026-10-01 as the shared `js/weather.js` overlay, not as renderer code: `index.js` `setStatic` applies it from the track theme plus the UTC day (one sky per track per day for everyone), as a canvas directly after `#racerCanvas` so the HUD stays above the rain, and `stop()` removes it. It is skipped under reduced motion and never touches what the server simulates. The two HUD strips carry `data-glass` (scene glass, see CLAUDE.md).
- **The page pushes numbers, not objects (2026-09-30).** `PoRacerCarState` is per-tick numbers only; names, paint, livery and a bot's trait are `PoRacerCarInfo` in `PoRacerStaticWorld.Roster`, sent on join and re-broadcast as `raceRoster` when a driver's paint arrives. `PoRacerPage` flattens each snapshot into one `double[]` (`Stride` = 14 per car; `index.js` `STRIDE` must match) and re-renders only when a lamp, place, lap or lap time changes. Speed and the two running clocks are written by `index.js` straight into `.race-speed`, `.race-clock` and `.race-lap-now`, which Blazor renders empty and must keep empty.
- **Stored laps are the server's (2026-09-30).** The sim already ran server-side but the score POST used to store the body's lap. `PoRacerRaceRegistry` now remembers each human's result for an hour when a race finishes (`VerifiedLapAsync`, keyed by claim identity + race code; in memory, and written through to `VerifiedResultStore` since 2026-10-01 so it survives a recycle) and the endpoint stores that lap, position and track; a code with no remembered race gets 422. The body still has to pass validation and only says which race is meant.
- **The race loop runs on wall time.** `PoRacerRaceService` steps the sim as many fixed 20 ms ticks as real time has passed (capped at five). It used to step once per `PeriodicTimer` fire, which is about every 31 ms on Windows: the race ran in slow motion against its own lap clock, and on a busy dev box no bot finished a lap inside the 180 s cap. Pause (solo only, hub `SetPaused`) freezes the race clock; every lap time is measured on it.
- One page, three modes: `/poracer/1player`, `/poracer/multi` and `/poracer/demo` are all `PoRacerPage` (the separate lobby page is gone). The switch on the start card is the shared `ModeSwitch` with `Reload="false"`, because this page follows the address in place; `.racer-card` re-points the `--seg-*` properties to the Racer palette. The paint shop sits in the lobby card's `Picker` slot with the track selector, above the buttons. The Online tab holds the lobby seat; `gameStarted` navigates to `/poracer/multi?code=…` so a reload rejoins. The track is the host seat's `PoRacerLobbyPlayer.TrackId`. Solo options ride `PoRacerJoinOptions`: `trial` (no bots, records no win/loss) and the tier, which sets bot pace and, mostly, how much bots lift for corners (`PoRacerSim.DefaultBotCaution`; easy is the old bots, demo and online bots keep it). Every human drives the same car profile.
- Track knots live in `PoRacerCatalog` (Shared) with the medal laps; the server's `PoRacerTrackRegistry` and the client's track-card outlines both read them. Medals and tiers were calibrated together against the 2026-09-30 physics (the wall bounce now deflects away from the barrier; it used to turn the car into it), so a physics change moves both, and the bot finish times `PoRacerSimAiTests` pins.
- **What moves a car (2026-10-01), all in `PoRacerSim`.** Top speed is no longer a constant: `TopSpeed(c)` is MaxSpeed x boost (1.10) x tow (1.04) x damage (down to 0.90), and a car over it bleeds down instead of snapping; the bots' corner-speed target is scaled from the same number, or a bot brakes for its own boost. Boost comes from the pads (two per track, `PadAt(centerline, lapFraction)` in `PoRacerTrackRegistry`; every track had an empty list before) and from a drift: Space with the wheel turned at speed on tarmac rotates the car 1.5x, scrubs 1.1/s instead of the handbrake's 3.0/s, charges `DriftCharge`, and pays out on release at 0.3 or more. The tow goes to every human and, as before, only to the two `PrefersDrafting` bots. Damage is by impact speed (wall up to 0.12, car contact up to 0.06), not per tick of contact. These shifted bot times by a second or two and human laps by about one; re-measure the medals and tiers if you touch them again. `PoRacerCarState` gained `Drafting` and `Drift`; the HUD's Tow / Boost / Drift chips are rendered `hidden` by Blazor and shown by `index.js`, like the three clocks.
- **A seat nobody holds is driven (2026-10-01).** In a `multi-` race `PoRacerRaceService` puts the stand-in bot (`PoRacerSim.SetAutopilot`, the same `UpdateAi`) on any human seat with no bound connection, from the lights or from a disconnect, and takes it off on `BindPlayer`. A car the bot drove for even one tick is `BotDriven` for good and is left out of `Humans`, so its laps are never remembered for a score submit. Solo races are untouched.
- Skid marks, impact sparks, sand dust and the live half of the boost pads are `renderer.js` only (`stepFx` / `drawFx` / `drawPads`), derived from the interpolated cars it is already handed and drawn in world space under the cars; sparks come from `index.js` seeing a car's `damage` rise between snapshots. They are flat (no glow, no additive blend) and sparks, dust and the pad animation stand down under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The Racer palette is declared once on `.racer-root` (`--racer-*` in `PoRacerPage.razor.css`) and the other PoRacer stylesheets read it.
- `PoRacerRaceRegistry` owns separate solo, demo and multiplayer races. Multiplayer rounds get unique codes; claim-derived identities bind lobby seats to race connections. Snapshots identify the local car only in the join response.
- Leaderboards rank best completed laps. `PoRacerScoreDto.BestLapSeconds` retains the JSON name `totalTimeSeconds`, and Azure retains its `TotalTimeSeconds` column. Existing values are preserved, not multiplied or divided to infer old race semantics.
- Score writes use the shared highscore rate limiter; reads use `/api/leaderboards/{key}`, one board per track: `poracer` (Grand Prix, the key its rows always had), `poracer-neonskyline`, `poracer-desertdustway`. The page passes the raced track's key as GameShell's `GameKey`; until 2026-10-01 only the Grand Prix partition was ever read, so the other two tracks' laps were stored and shown nowhere. Retryable writes persist through `PendingScoreStore` using source-generated serialization metadata; a parked lap is only accepted while the registry still remembers its race (an hour; across a recycle too, as long as Table Storage was reachable when the race finished).
- Test caps count methods, not theory rows. No fifth test tier is introduced.
