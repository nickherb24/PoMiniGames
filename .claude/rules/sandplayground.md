# SandPlayground

- **SandPlayground** (client-only: `Games/SandPlayground`, `js/sand-playground/`; overhauled 2026-09-30). An 800x600 cellular world on the GPU (`sand-playground-physics.glsl.js`: SUPPORT → RELAX → MOVE → FALL → SURGE per sub-step) with a CPU byte mirror the rigid bodies, ordnance and tools read. **The shaders are strings compiled in the browser, so `dotnet build` cannot see a typo**: a dropped line in COMPOSITE left the page on the error boundary from 2026-09-16 to 2026-09-30; `SandPlaygroundUiTests` (E2E-UI) now boots the engine, and `?debug` installs `window.__sandPlayground` (never for players: `detonate`/`paint` would beat every challenge). No backtick may appear inside a shader source, comments included; it ends the template literal. **Support is 2D:** sand is propped from below and arches sideways about a dozen cells, so in this side view a level gallery's roof span is its *length*. Scene builders must carve only what that can roof: shafts, short galleries sloping down to a dead end, round chambers (`carveNest`). The old wandering galleries stoped up through the lake bed within 3 s of every load. Three rules in SUPPORT that look tunable are load-bearing: the yield limit includes packed-ground cohesion (`+ self.a * 2.0`; without it no dry cell with air under it can stand), wet weakening applies only to loose sediment (`self.a < 0.5`; applied to packed ground a lake soaks through its own bed), and the grain fraction treats `> 0.95` as "none" (half-float lands a byte value a hair under the integer, and the stray 0.999 turned every drained grain into glass, which was the only thing sealing the lake before). Readback goes through an RGBA8 COPY pass, not a FLOAT `readPixels` (that and its 1.9M-iteration convert loop cost most of the frame). Juice particles are transform-feedback (16k slots, `PUPDATE`); the smoke field, sky (`u_sky` from the engine's `sun`/rain), x-ray views (`u_view`), sharp upscale (`u_out`) and reset transition (`u_melt`) are all in `sand-playground-render.glsl.js`. `sand-playground-audio.js` hangs off `PoAudioBus` and must never close that context. A lost WebGL context rebuilds everything from the mirror (`setupGl`). Blazor owns only the dock, tray and menu; the engine reports through one callback, `OnEngine(kind, value)`, and interop passes primitives, never anonymous objects (the trimmer may drop their properties). Worlds are `(preset, seed)`; `?world=&seed=` (or `seed=daily`) opens one. Challenge bests and the three save slots are localStorage only; nothing is submitted to the server.
