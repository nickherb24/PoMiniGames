# PoSports

- **PoSports** (`Features/PoSports`, `Games/PoSports`, `js/posports/`). **Stored solo times are server-replayed (2026-09-30):** a solo submit carries `Inputs`, the run's key log (`runlog.js`: two legs joined by `|`, each `F;v.v.v…`, `v` = base-36 of `tick*5+code`, tick = fixed steps already run on that leg), and `PoSportsRunVerifier` feeds it through `PoSportsSim` — the sim that already times online races — and stores the time *it* gets; the posted times are only compared and logged, and a submit with no usable log gets 422. `Inputs` and `Daily` are request-only fields on `PoSportsHighScore` (the descriptor's `Sanitize` never copies them). `tests/Shared/Fixtures/posports-run-proofs.txt` holds logs recorded with the browser engine's own modules and the leg times JS got; the verifier reproduces them to 1e-9 — regenerate, never hand-edit, if the stride model changes on purpose. The daily meet's board is the same table and descriptor in partition `posports_daily_<yyyyMMdd>` (UTC day, `GET /api/posports/highscores/daily`). Everything a player races against is local (`records.js`, localStorage `posports.records.v1`): per-leg and meet bests, the ghost (the best meet's key log, replayed through its own `SequenceTracker` + lane state — never one of `lanes`), field-event bests, the daily streak, key bindings. `game.js`: the stumble hit-stop scales how much *real* time feeds the fixed-step accumulator, local modes only, so leg times stay on the sim clock. The photo-finish slow motion (with its camera push-in, wash and 0.45 s frame hold) was removed on 2026-10-01 at the user's request; the PHOTO FINISH result banner stayed. The same day the day-meet heat shimmer came out of `postfx.js`: its band sat 20 to 50% down the frame, which is the top lane, so whoever ran there got wavy hurdles. Do not put a screen-space distortion band back over the track. Weather (2026-10-01) is the shared `js/weather.js` overlay from the meet's seed, rain and snow only: its fog is a blur over the whole stage, which is the same mistake. It sits after `.ps-postfx`, is skipped under reduced motion and is never part of the sim or the run log. Online sound and dressing are snapshot *edges* in `applySnapshot` (the server sends state, not events), and the podium snapshot arrives only on `raceFinished` — the page must forward it or the canvas never leaves the race. `input.js` `LAYOUTS` is module-global and survives the page session, so the page always passes both keymaps; `KeyLabel` in the page mirrors `keyLabel` in `input.js`. `events.js` (long jump, javelin, relay) is local-only: there is no server model, so those are bests, not board rows. `#posports-container` holds two canvases (the 2D one and `.ps-postfx`, a WebGL2 grade gated off by `data-gfx=low` and reduced motion), so tests select `canvas.ps-canvas`. The page ends on `GameOverModal` again (2026-10-02, user call; from 2026-09-30 it had a results card of its own with Again / Change runner): the splits, bests and daily streak are the modal's summary, `GameOverDelayMs` 3000 lets the podium play first, and only the two-leg meet passes a `GameKey` (field events have no board). The lobby opens on the intro card again and takes its seat when the card is dismissed. The solo intro, both 2P sides and the lobby share `PoSportsRunnerPicker`, and both pages share `PoSportsHowTo`.
