# PoVoxelStrike

- **PoVoxelStrike** (`Features/PoVoxelStrike`, `Games/PoVoxelStrike`, `js/povoxelstrike/`; siege a destructible voxel fortress, take the chalice). **One page, one start card (2026-09-30):** `PoVoxelStrikePage` hosts Solo / Online / Demo; the lobby is the Online tab (`PoVoxelStrikeLobbyPage` and the `/povoxelstrike/multi/{code}` handoff route are gone) and an online run starts in place. The engine is built in state `'ready'` behind the card (rendered, nothing simulated: the clock and the wall guns used to run under the old click-to-play veil) and `enter()` from the card's button is the gesture that takes Pointer Lock. A pad or a touch screen cannot lock, so they get **free control** (`input.js`; `_controlling()` is the one "is the player driving" test, and pause there is a button, not lock loss). The page reuses its instance across `/povoxelstrike/{Mode}` routes, so it re-reads its mode in `OnParametersSet`. **Wins are priced by a flag.** The chalice bonus decays (`winBonus` in game.js = `PoVoxelStrikeScore.WinBonus`, 25,000 − 40/s, floor 5,000: two copies, change both) and the endpoint's plausibility ceiling (`PoVoxelStrikeRunRequest.ScoreCeiling`) only includes it when the submit says `Won`. Before that every win was a 400: the ceiling had no bonus term and, with roaming enemies off, no kill slack. The client computes the bonus from the same rounded seconds it submits. A run is also recorded as `GameResult.Win` now, not always Loss. **Daily Siege** is the same descriptor in a per-day partition (`povoxelstrike_{yyyy-MM-dd}`, the day re-formatted server-side before it reaches the OData filter; seed = FNV-1a of the UTC date, client-side and unverified); `PlayerDataService` scans every partition of that table for that reason. `?seed=<hex>` replays an arena. **Online is still co-presence, plus three relayed facts:** carves (`Carve` on the lockstep hub → `Weapon.applyRemote`, keyed by the index into the seed-built structure list; its own message because input batches are latest-wins), the win (`ClaimChalice` → `squadWon`), and `Down` on the input (0 HP with a live squadmate is down-not-dead; each client decides its own revive from the positions it is sent). Hub args are flat primitives on purpose (no DTO to register). **Interop arg order is a contract** (game.js header ↔ the page's `[JSInvokable]`s): `OnHudTick` no longer carries heat/blast (the crosshair rings are their only readout) and `OnGameOver`/`OnVictory` grew guns-silenced, guns-total and damage-taken at the end. **Modes:** Survival is `EnemyManager` `opts.enabled` (was the constant `ENEMIES_ENABLED`), solo only. **Feel layer, all render/audio-only:** `avatar.js` (voxel figure for player and peers, name tags), wall collision on the chase camera (one ray per frame along the boom; pulled in at once, eased out), `weather.js` (seeded clear/rain/fog/storm; rain is one camera-wrapped `LineSegments`), hot carve rims and wet stone in `materials.js` (`onBeforeCompile` on every voxel material, one shared program key; chunk names `project_vertex`/`emissivemap_fragment` are pinned to three 0.165), heat shimmer / lens dust / low-HP desaturation / lightning folded into vfx.js's Combine pass, hit-stop and the victory beat as a simulation time scale (`sdt`; the run clock stays on real `dt`), `clip.js` (a 5 s WebM started when a collapse detaches: a WebM can't be cut afterwards). `audio.js` has no music of its own any more; it drives `PoMusicDirector.tension`. Two old bugs fixed in passing: the frictionless player never braked when the keys were released, and the canvas wrapper took its width from the status sentence under it (gone), so `.pvs-wrap` now has an explicit width. Touch controls are verified in an emulated phone only, never on hardware. The win-pricing check lives inside `PoVoxelStrikeEndpointsTests.RunRoutes_AreAuthGated_AndPriceAWinByItsFlag` (E2E-API), because the Unit tier had no room for it on 2026-09-30.
