# PoEcosystem

- **PoEcosystem got its API slice on 2026-09-14** (`Features/PoEcosystem`): three cloud world slots per identity, a public gallery, a decade chronicle and cloud thoughts. A world is an opaque gzip'd snapshot the browser engine packs itself (`js/poecosystem/sim/persistence/codec.js`, typed arrays as tagged base64) — the server stores bytes in blob container `poecosystem-worlds` and metadata in table `PoEcosystemWorlds` (partition = hash of the claim id; sharing mirrors a row into the `public` partition keyed by an 8-char code, so the gallery is one partition query). `EcosystemWorldStore` degrades to empty/null on storage failure. Do **not** put `.Accepts("application/gzip")` on the PUT: `IAcceptsMetadata` takes part in endpoint *selection*, so a mismatched Content-Type lands on a synthetic 415 endpoint with no auth metadata and an anonymous caller gets the antiforgery 403 instead of 401 (a route-exists leak the E2E-API contract test catches). AI game key `ecosystem` (own resilience partition) plus task key `ecosystem.thought` (cheap model); `PoEcosystem:Features:UseMockAI` is in `TestBudgetGuard`. Cloud thoughts are throttled to one per 20 s in `thoughtBridge.js` and capped per session in the viewer, because each spends the caller's daily allowance. Engine-side, the same commit made the render frame stride 13 (five base traits ride along for the evolution tint; 16 since 2026-10-01 — see the consolidation note below), added `sim/creatures/lineage.js` (family trees, watch-list, player naming — all snapshotted) and `sim/behavior/tech.js` (the tribe's Camp → Fire → Palisade → Farming → Watchtower ladder, deterministic, no RNG draws), and `render/director.js` + `render/pip.js` (auto-director camera; Document Picture-in-Picture with a 2D mirror canvas, video PiP fallback). The snapshot round-trip divergence noted at the time (~115 ticks after restore) was fixed on 2026-09-23 — see the observation note below. The GFX pass that followed lives in `render/sky.js` (dome + clouds, camera-parented) and `render/materials.js` (`enhanceLambert`: one `onBeforeCompile` hook for rim light, world-space mottle and wind sway, shared by creatures, flora and props; `materialClock`/`materialDetail` are the only uniforms the renderer drives). Keep MeshLambertMaterial — the flat-shaded low-poly look is the art direction, and Lambert has lit per fragment since three r155, which is what the injected bump normals rely on. Shader chunk names (`opaque_fragment`, `normal_fragment_begin`) are pinned to three 0.165.0 in the import map; bumping three means re-checking them.
- **Palette and motion are the app's, not the island's (2026-10-01).** The dashboard's view settings lost their "Colours" and "Reduce motion" rows; `index.js` `paletteInUse()` / `motionReduced()` read only `<html data-colorsafe>` / `<html data-motion>` (the settings sheet), and the `palette` / `reducedMotion` prefs an older build stored are ignored. `setPalette` / `setReducedMotion` are gone from the engine, the facade and `PoEcosystemInteropService`. Graphics quality and key bindings are still the island's own. The HUD's 🔊 is the island's ambience on/off, not a second mute.
- **PoEcosystem is observed, never steered (user call, 2026-09-23).** No feature may let the viewer change the world: the HUD's Divine Decree console was removed, and so were the powers/favor system and the "mirror my local weather" toggle built alongside it. Everything that changes the island is the sim's own doing — weather (`sim/events/weather.js`), contagion (`sim/creatures/disease.js`), named varieties, and the Chieftain Council's treaties, which the *sim's* diplomacy triggers (the page only relays them to `/treaty` when signed in). Viewer-side additions are observational: the dashboard Timeline (sim `landmarks` + one `yearHistory` row per year, sent as a `history` message only when it grows), Field notes (`Models/EcoMilestones.cs`, localStorage), Shannon/Pielou biodiversity, iNaturalist/Wikipedia field-guide cards (`host/naturalist.js`, fetched with `credentials: 'omit'`, never through the app `HttpClient`), render-only birds and fish (`render/fauna.js`, deliberately not a fifth sim species), and view settings (quality tier, Okabe–Ito palette via `data-poeco-palette`, reduced motion, rebindable keys, gamepad). `/api/ecosystem/decree` is gone (2026-10-01: endpoint, service method, schema, mock, task key and DTOs — nothing had called it since the console was removed). GFX pass 2 (2026-09-23) is render-only too: `render/trails.js` (desire paths + fresh tracks from interpolated creature rows, never the sim), `render/grass.js` (camera-riding instanced blades on a hooked Lambert, high/medium only), weather-driven wet ground/puddles/lowland snow and night territory borders in `terrainMesh.js`, aurora + meteors in `sky.js` and bioluminescent surf in `water.js` (both chosen by hashing the world seed + date in `renderer.js`, so they belong to the world, not the viewer), heat shimmer/shockwave/mood grade folded into the one atmosphere pass (lens rain was deleted on 2026-10-01, user call), creature voices and the trait-composed leitmotif (`render/leitmotif.js`), chronicle cards (`render/chronicle.js`, filed into the HUD chip with a View Transition) and the Island Reel (`render/reel.js`, two staggered MediaRecorders on a 2D mirror canvas — a WebM can't be cut mid-stream). The snapshot round-trip is deterministic again: the Box–Muller spare is saved in RNG state, diplomacy draws from its own `tribes` stream, and the caravan timer and building ids/links are persisted — `node` can drive `sim/world.js` headlessly to check it.
- **PoEcosystem features/GFX/consolidation pass (2026-10-01).** `/poecosystem/1player` resumes your island; `/poecosystem/demo`, a `?island=CODE` visit and an opened time-machine decade are all **ephemeral** (`simRuntime` `ephemeral`: never autosaves, never writes keyframes) — the page hardcoded demo before, so every visit autosaved a fresh island over the last one. The viewer's `ReadOnly` (visiting or in the past) is what disables chronicle writes, cloud saves and wagers. **Never put `data-glass` on `.poeco-hud`**: it is a full-screen element, so glassFx blurred the whole island and paid a canvas readback every 220 ms (≈2 fps → ≈50 headless once removed). Frame stride is **16** (`sim/frame.js`: hunger, health, a state byte with `C_SICK`/`C_WATCHED`), which feeds the naturalist lenses (`creatureMeshes.js` `LENS`, `materials.js` `materialLens`) and kin threads (`render/kin.js`); a stride change touches `frame.js`, `creatureMeshes.draw` and every reader of the row in `renderer.js`. New world state is additive and RNG-free, so the snapshot round-trip stays deterministic: heat grids (deaths/kills/sick, 50×50, the minimap's data layers) and the `journal` of sagas (written through `note('saga', …)`, so the chronicle survives a resume, a cloud load and can be printed). The time machine is one IndexedDB keyframe per decade (`idb.js`, 8 kept, writes chained through `keyframeQueue` — two overlapping writes lost year 10); opening one saves the present first. `materials.js` `cloudShadowGlsl` duplicates `sky.js`'s cloud noise constants (height 96, scale 0.006, drift) — change them together or the shadows leave their clouds. The dashboard is four tabs and a gear (Island, Life, Tribes, Worlds); `TimelinePanel` holds the only population chart, the inspector holds the family tree, and the ticker (`chronicle.js` `ticker`) is the one notification channel — no toasts. Deleted: `Sparkline`, `PopulationChart`, `LineagePanel`, `DynastyTree` (its Phenotype tab showed hard-coded numbers), `CloudPanel`, and `TerritoryMinimap`'s fake Resources/Danger layers. Wagers (`Models/EcoWager.cs`) and watch alerts (`Models/EcoAlerts.cs`) are localStorage only, deliberately: seeds are deterministic, so a server wager board would be a lookup table. Gallery thumbnails are drawn client-side from the seed (`index.js` `islandThumb`), not uploaded. The finder releases pointer lock and re-takes it only if it held it when it opened (`state.relock`). `gameCues.js` still has two `poecosystem:` keys, so the first block (the old god-power cues) is shadowed dead weight.
- **PoEcosystem: solid ground (2026-10-01).** Creatures no longer walk through things or stand on cliffs. `sim/terrain/ground.js` derives two per-tile grids from world state (never saved, no RNG, rebuilt when flora or buildings change): a footprint radius (trunks, stumps and bushes 0.45 m, deliberately under half a tile so two neighbouring trees always leave a gap; huts, campfire, towers and tribe buildings larger) and a `closed` mask (a tile whose corners differ by more than 2.0 m is a cliff; cut-off pockets and tiles under a building are closed too). `steering.js` walks a blocked creature round a footprint's rim on the side its yaw already leans, so yaw is the only memory and the snapshot gained nothing; the round-trip is still identical through both save paths. Targets that stay put (grass, bushes, trees, carcasses, remembered tiles) need a walkable straight line or are ignored; prey, mates and parents are NOT held to that, because with it the wolves died out in 11 of 32 twenty-year runs (7 without, 5 before). Things are used from their edge: home is the hut wall, water is any shore tile. A creature somewhere illegal (old saves, a hut rising on it) is walked to the nearest standing room. The palisade is now solid for humans too (they use the gates), which reverses the old rule; it is one line in `steering.js` `blocked()`. Walled lakes are no longer drinkable, so walks to water are longer and the tribe runs about a fifth smaller. Canopies, hut roofs and the physics rocks and logs are not solid (a solid canopy walls the forest; reading a physics body would break determinism). Known: a villager can ping-pong in a pocket between overlapping village footprints about once per hundred sim-years. Render side: four-legged bodies pitch and roll to the ground under their feet (`creatureMeshes.js`); a lake is drawn with its bed 0.8 m under the surface and a lake mask turns off foam, caustics and sun glint there (the sim flattens a lake to exactly the water plane, so depth read 0 everywhere and shore foam plus glint painted the whole lake white); the sea is unchanged. Also fixed in `world.js` restore: the cloud (JSON) save wrote `Infinity` plan distances as null, and a restored world diverged within five ticks on some seeds.
