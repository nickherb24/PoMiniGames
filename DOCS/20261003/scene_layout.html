Level & Arena Setup

Where things are in each game: bounds, spawn points, hazards and trigger zones. Snapshot 2026-10-03. Drawings are top-down and to scale, generated from the constants in the source files named under each one.

Tier 1 · Quick look
Seven games have a physical arena; the two board games have a grid, and the quiz and joke games have none. No arena uses a navigation mesh: every agent steers by simple probes or follows a line. Arenas are built in code (or loaded from .glb models), not laid out in a scene editor, so "moving a spawn point" means changing a number.

Tier 3 · Setup guide

Engine setup tips

Collision layers

Three games use the cannon-es physics library and set collision groups in code. This is the same idea as Unity's Layer Collision Matrix.

GameLayerCollides with
PoBrawlG_ROOT (live fighter)fighter, ragdoll, prop
G_RAGDOLLragdoll, arena, fighter, prop
G_ARENA (posts, rope walls)ragdoll, prop
G_PROP (crates)fighter, prop, ragdoll, arena
PoVoxelStrikePLAYER_GROUPworld, player
WORLD_GROUPeverything
PoEcosystemG_TERRAIN, G_RAGDOLL, G_PROPCosmetic bodies only. Living creatures have no body and are never in the matrix.
  • Live PoBrawl fighters do not collide with the arena layer. They are kept inside the ring by a position clamp, and kept apart from each other by a manual 0.95 m rule.
  • PoVoxelStrike monsters have no physics body at all. They test wall voxels directly, which is why they cannot be pushed.

Tags

There is no tag system. An object's role comes from which list it is stored in (enemies, guns, debris, props). If you port a scene to Unity, the natural tags are Player, Enemy, Projectile, Hazard, Goal and Cover.

Navigation

  • No navigation mesh anywhere. There are no nav bounds to bake.
  • PoVoxelStrike monsters try seven headings and take the first one clear of walls.
  • Racers follow a centre line (PoRacer) or a look-ahead point on the road (PoCabinet).
  • Island creatures step over a height map and try ±45°, ±90° and ±135° when blocked.
  • Arena units bend their path round pillars and walls with a 1.2 m look-ahead.
